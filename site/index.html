Beta

Manatable

Collect, build and battle.

Scan your cards, see what each printing is worth, build decks from what you own and keep score at the table. Orin, the owl, helps along the way.

  • Camera scanner. Point your phone at a card, or a whole stack. It reads the name, the exact printing and whether it is foil, then adds it.
  • Binders like yours. Pages of 2×2, 3×3 or 4×4 pockets that match your real binder. Scan a whole page at once and drag cards between pockets.
  • What it is worth. Every printing has its own price, so a showcase foil is not counted like the plain one. Watch the value move day by day.

Nopehook

Check it before you bite.

Paste a sketchy text, email, link or screenshot and get a clear answer: likely scam, suspicious, or looks fine, with the red flags explained in plain English.

  • Clear verdicts. Likely scam, suspicious, or looks fine, with the red flags explained in plain English and the next step to take.
  • Built on official warnings. Checks are grounded in public scam alerts from agencies like the FTC and FBI, and the scam list updates every week.
  • Links checked too. Web addresses in a message are checked against known dangerous sites before you tap them.

Unbadged

Say it without the name tag.

An anonymous break room for retail, warehouse, trades and service workers. Vent, compare pay and swap stories with people who get it.

  • Boards for every job. Boards by job type and company, each with General, Vent and Pay & Schedules chats.
  • Pay Check. See what a job really pays, using public pay data adjusted by what members report.
  • Anonymous by design. No real names. Faces in public photos are blurred automatically before anyone sees them.

Firefly Dash

One tap. Ten worlds.

A one-tap pixel art flyer. Guide a firefly through ten glowing worlds, collect sparks and build your loadout of abilities.

  • Ten worlds. From the Moonlit Swamp at the start to the Heart of Light at 4,200 meters, each with its own look and sound.
  • New course every run. Courses are generated fresh each time, so no two runs are the same.
  • Spark Shop. Spend the sparks you collect on power-ups, upgrades and glows.
