Manatable

Collect, build and battle.

Beta

Manatable brings Commander night into one app: scan your cards with your phone camera, see what every printing is worth, build decks from what you own, and keep life at the table. Orin, the built-in owl, helps along the way.

Join the betaFollow on TikTok

From card box to game night

  1. Scan your cards

    Point your phone at a card. It reads the name and exact printing and adds it to your collection.

  2. Build and check decks

    See what you can build from what you own, then get a deep dive and coaching on every deck.

  3. Play game night

    Track life and commander damage at the table. Every finished game saves to your stats.

Meet Orin

Orin is the owl who lives in Manatable. He reads your cards in the scanner, learns from your fixes, finds decks you can build and upgrades from cards you already own, and answers questions in plain words, even with no signal.

Take a look

Everything for Commander night

Camera scanner

Point your phone at a card, or a whole stack. It reads the name, the exact printing and whether it is foil, then adds it.

Binders like yours

Pages of 2×2, 3×3 or 4×4 pockets that match your real binder. Scan a whole page at once and drag cards between pockets.

What it is worth

Every printing has its own price, so a showcase foil is not counted like the plain one. Watch the value move day by day.

Decks from what you own

Check off the precons you own, build around any card, and let Orin suggest upgrades from cards you already have.

Life counter

Life, commander damage and counters for two to six players, with each seat showing its commander art.

Ask Orin

Ask in plain words: cheap green ramp, board wipes I own, what ward means. It works with no signal.

Questions

Is this an official Magic: The Gathering app?

No. Manatable is an independent app and is not affiliated with, approved or endorsed by Wizards of the Coast.

How does the scanner work?

It reads the card name, set code and collector number with your phone camera, then matches the exact printing so prices are right.

Does it need internet?

Card images and prices load online and are saved on your phone, so cards you have already seen still show offline.

When can I get it?

Manatable is in beta on iPhone through TestFlight. Ask for a spot with the Join the beta button and we will email you an invite.

Who is Orin?

Orin is the built-in owl helper. He powers the scanner, deck ideas and Ask Orin, and he runs on your phone, so your card pictures stay on it.
