Manatable

Collect, build and battle.

In testing

Manatable brings a Commander game night into one app: scan your collection with your phone camera, keep life and commander damage, save every game, and dig into your decks.

Join the betaFollow on TikTok

From card box to game night

  1. Scan your cards

    Point your phone at a card. It reads the name and exact printing and adds it to your collection.

  2. Build and check decks

    See what you can build from what you own, then get a deep dive and coaching on every deck.

  3. Play game night

    Track life and commander damage at the table. Every finished game saves to your stats.

Take a look

Everything for Commander night

Camera scanner

Point your phone at a card and it reads the name and printing, then adds it to your collection.

Life counter

Life, commander damage and counters for two to six players, with each seat showing its commander.

Game night stats

Finished games save automatically, with win rates, favorite commanders and rivalries.

Deck deep dive

Card types, mana curve, draw odds and a coach that suggests swaps and cards to add.

Build from what you own

Find commanders you can build from cards already in your collection, and what it costs to finish.

Collection value

See what your cards are worth, spot valuable cards sitting in bulk, and watch prices.

Questions

Is this an official Magic: The Gathering app?

No. Manatable is an independent app and is not affiliated with, approved or endorsed by Wizards of the Coast.

How does the scanner work?

It reads the card name, set code and collector number with your phone camera, then matches the exact printing so prices are right.

Does it need internet?

Card images and prices load online and are saved on your phone, so cards you have already seen still show offline.

When can I get it?

Manatable is in testing. Follow @moonshiftstudio on TikTok for the launch.
