Firefly Dash

One tap. Ten worlds.

Play free now

Firefly Dash is a one-tap pixel art game. Tap to fly a firefly through the gaps, collect rare sparks, and push deeper through ten worlds, from the Moonlit Swamp all the way to the Heart of Light.

Play now

Easy to learn, hard to put down

  1. Tap to fly

    One tap lifts your firefly. Thread it through the gaps without touching anything.

  2. Grab the sparks

    Sparks are rare. Collect them as you fly and push into new worlds.

  3. Power up

    Spend sparks in the Spark Shop, set your four-ability loadout and go farther next run.

Take a look

Ten worlds of glowing pixel art

Ten worlds

From the Moonlit Swamp at the start to the Heart of Light at 4,200 meters, each with its own look and sound.

New course every run

Courses are generated fresh each time, so no two runs are the same.

Spark Shop

Spend the sparks you collect on power-ups, upgrades and glows.

Abilities and loadouts

Eight abilities with a four-slot loadout, and new ones unlock as you reach new worlds.

Plays on any phone

Runs right in your phone browser. Add it to your home screen to play like an app.

One-hand friendly

Left- or right-hand layouts and three sound styles.

Firefly Dash is free to play in your phone browser now. App Store and Google Play versions come later.

Questions

Is Firefly Dash free?

Yes. It is free to play in your phone browser.

Do I need to download anything?

No. Open it in your browser, then use Add to Home Screen to keep it on your phone like an app.

How many worlds are there?

Ten, from the Moonlit Swamp at the start to the Heart of Light at 4,200 meters.

Will it be on the App Store and Google Play?

Store versions are planned for later. The QR code on this page will take you to them when they are ready.
