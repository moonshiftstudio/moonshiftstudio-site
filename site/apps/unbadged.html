Questions

Who is Unbadged for?

Adults 18 and older who work in retail, warehouses, the trades or service jobs.

Is it really anonymous?

You never use your real name, and faces in public photos are blurred automatically before anyone sees them.

How do I get in?

Unbadged is invite-only during the beta. Email hello@unbadged.app to ask for an invite.

How do I report a problem?

Email reports@unbadged.app with what happened and where in the app you saw it.
