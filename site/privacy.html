Privacy

Last updated October 20, 2026.

This website

moonshiftstudio.com does not use cookies, ads, analytics or tracking. Like most websites, our hosting provider keeps standard server logs (such as IP address and browser type) to keep the site running and secure.

The testers list

If you join the testers list, we save the email address you give us, the apps you picked, your phone type and the date you signed up. We use it only to send test invites and launch news for those apps. We never sell it or share it for marketing. To be removed, email hello@moonshiftstudio.com and we'll delete your entry.

QR codes

When you scan a Moonshift QR code, the page checks whether you're on an iPhone or an Android phone so it can open the right app store. That check happens on your phone and nothing is saved.

Playing Firefly Dash on this site

The game only loads when you tap Play. It runs from fireflydash.moonshiftstudio.com.

Our apps

Each Moonshift app has its own privacy policy, which will be published here before that app launches.
